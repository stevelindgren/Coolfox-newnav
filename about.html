What separates Cool Fox from the rest?

Cool Fox Trucks builds the highest quality refrigerated vans, insulated vans, and trucks from our production facility in the New York City metropolitan area. We make a tremendous effort to ensure every project we complete has an OEM level of fit and finish as well as unrivaled longevity.

Engineered For Long Life

Built for long-term refrigerated vehicle performance

Cool Fox Trucks was built to address a common problem in the refrigerated vehicle industry. Many refrigerated vans and trucks are designed to look acceptable at delivery, but are not engineered for long-term temperature stability, durability, or daily commercial use.

From insulation methods to material selection and installation standards, we take a long-term approach to every refrigerated van and truck we build. Our focus is on consistent temperature control, structural integrity, and real-world performance over the life of the vehicle, not shortcuts that lead to early failure or costly repairs.

Build standards that hold up in daily operation

Our process is built around repeatable installation standards, documented quality checkpoints, and practical field experience. We design each refrigerated van and truck for operators who rely on consistent temperature control and durable construction every day.

From insulation execution and material fitment to finish quality and final delivery checks, each build is reviewed for long-term reliability. The goal is fewer service issues, stronger thermal performance, and vehicles that stay dependable over time.

Skilled employees

Our production facility is staffed by experienced technicians who specialize in building refrigerated vans and trucks. Every installation is completed in-house with attention to detail, accountability, and craftsmanship at every stage of the build process.

Proven Design

Each refrigerated van and truck we build is based on proven designs refined through years of real-world use. From insulation layouts to refrigeration system integration, our builds are engineered for durability, resale value, and consistent temperature performance.

Continuous Improvement

Cool Fox Trucks works closely with customers across food, seafood, and temperature-sensitive industries. Feedback from operators directly informs how we refine our refrigerated vans and trucks to perform better in real operating conditions.

Hands-On Leadership

Our management team stays actively involved throughout the build process, maintaining clear communication with customers from design through delivery. Every refrigerated vehicle is built with accountability, honesty, and long-term performance in mind.

Quality In, Quality Out

Only high-quality materials and components are used in our refrigerated vans and insulated trucks. We do not cut corners or rely on sub-standard hardware that leads to corrosion, premature failure, or reduced vehicle lifespan.

Built In-House

Every refrigerated van and truck is built in-house at our Copiague, New York production facility. Keeping the build process under one roof ensures consistent quality control, installation standards, and accountability from design through delivery.

Who We Serve

Refrigerated vehicles built for real operators

Cool Fox Trucks works with businesses that depend on reliable temperature-controlled transportation to protect their products and their reputation. Our refrigerated vans and trucks are built for operators who cannot afford temperature loss, equipment failure, or downtime.

We serve customers across seafood and fish markets, restaurants and catering, ice cream and frozen desserts, grocery distribution, farm-to-table operations, and medical and pharmaceutical transport throughout Long Island and the surrounding region.

How We Build

Designed and built without shortcuts

The performance of a refrigerated van or truck depends on how it is built. Insulation coverage, material selection, and installation standards all directly affect temperature stability, durability, and long-term reliability.

Cool Fox Trucks builds refrigerated vehicles with an emphasis on OEM-level fit and finish, premium materials, and in-house installation standards. Every build is designed to perform consistently under real-world commercial use, not just look finished on delivery day.

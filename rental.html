Refrigerated Rentals

Refrigerated Van & Trailer Rental

We offer late model refrigerated vans for rent along with custom built refrigerated trailers for rent on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis.

Van Rentals

Late-model refrigerated van rental

Cool Fox refrigerated rental vans are built on brand new Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Ford Transit vehicles and feature modern, commercial-grade refrigeration systems.

Each rental van includes standby power, allowing the refrigeration unit to run on electric power when parked by plugging into a standard 110v outlet, making the van usable as a walk-in refrigerator for secure, temperature-controlled storage. Daily, weekly, and special situation rates are available on all refrigerated van rentals.

Call us – (631) 608-2112 or email us today rental@coolfoxtrucks.com

  • Daily – $250 plus 25 cents per mile
  • Weekly – (5 day) $750 plus 25 cents per mile
  • $250 security deposit on reservation, non refundable if cancelled
  • Commercial vehicle insurance required as well, please click here for more information
  • NYS Sales and Rental tax applicable

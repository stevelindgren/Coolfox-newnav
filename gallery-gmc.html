GMC Savana Refrigerated Van Builds

The GMC Savana is one of the last vans built on a full frame chassis, offering high payload and class leading towing capacity. Ideal for refrigerated van conversions and refrigerated delivery routes, the Extended length Savana paired with Cool Fox’s 3 inch insulation package provides a 126 inch cargo area with space for up to three pallets.

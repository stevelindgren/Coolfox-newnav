Mercedes Sprinter Refrigerated Van Builds

Offered by both Mercedes-Benz and Freightliner, the Sprinter is a popular choice for customers looking for a refrigerated van conversion. Cool Fox Trucks works closely with each customer’s exact specifications to build the ideal refrigerated vehicle for their needs. Custom Sprinter van builds can include split cargo compartments for dry and cold storage, as well as dual temperature zones for frozen and fresh products, all within a single van.

Ford Cargo Area Dimensions

Cargo area dimensions are based on a standard Cool Fox refrigerated van build with 3" insulation. Measurements reflect interior cargo space only and do not include refrigeration equipment. Final dimensions may vary depending on the refrigeration system and vehicle configuration.

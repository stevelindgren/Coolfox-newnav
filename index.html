Common Questions

Refrigerated Vehicle FAQs

Everything you need to know about our refrigerated vans and trucks, including build options, temperature performance, industries served, and what to expect when working with Cool Fox.

What types of refrigerated vans do you offer?

We build a full range of refrigerated vans and trucks, from compact city delivery vehicles to large long-haul configurations. Our builds are tailored to your cargo, route demands, and temperature requirements.

How do I choose the right refrigerated van for my needs?

Choosing the right refrigerated van depends on what you transport, how much volume you need, and how far you drive. Our team works with you to recommend the best vehicle platform and refrigeration setup based on your operation.

What temperature ranges can your refrigerated vans maintain?

Our refrigerated vans support chilled and frozen temperature ranges, including multi-zone configurations when needed. Each system is designed to maintain consistent temperatures for your specific products.

Can I customize the refrigeration units in my van?

Yes. Every refrigerated van build is customizable, including refrigeration units, insulation, shelving, flooring, and cargo layout to match your exact business needs.

What warranty and support options are available?

Our vans come with a robust warranty and support network designed for businesses operating along the Eastern Coast. With comprehensive coverage and 24/7 support, we ensure that you receive prompt assistance whenever needed, minimizing downtime and enhancing productivity.

What vehicle brands do you build refrigerated vans on?

We build refrigerated vans and trucks on leading OEM platforms including Ford, Mercedes-Benz, GM, and RAM. We help you choose the best platform based on payload, reliability, and daily usage.

How long does it take to build a refrigerated van or truck?

Build timelines vary based on vehicle availability and customization level. Most refrigerated van builds are completed within 4-6 weeks after final specifications are approved.

Can you convert or retrofit an existing van with refrigeration?

In some cases, yes. We can evaluate your existing vehicle to determine if it is suitable for a refrigerated conversion based on size, condition, and power requirements.

How do I get pricing for a refrigerated van build?

Pricing depends on the vehicle platform, refrigeration system, and customization options. Contact our team to discuss your needs and receive a tailored quote.

Does CoolFox work with government grants?

Yes. Cool Fox works with aquaculture and seafood businesses pursuing grant-funded infrastructure upgrades by designing and building refrigerated vans and trucks that align with eligible refrigeration expenses. We do not administer or approve grants, but we provide compliant vehicle solutions that support cold-chain requirements. Read more about eligible aquaculture grant programs

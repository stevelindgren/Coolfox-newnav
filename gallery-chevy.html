Chevrolet Express

Chevrolet Express Refrigerated Van Builds

The Chevy Express owns a unique place in the van marketplace as it is one of the last to utilize a full frame style chassis, this leads to a higher payload capacity and by far the highest towing capacity of any van available today. Close to the ideal refrigerated van in size and perfect for urban delivery routes where maneuverability and durability matter, the Express is best optioned in the “Extended” length version when paired with Cool Fox’s 3” insulation package. This increase in length yields a cargo area length of 126 inches, enough room for 3 pallets of product.
